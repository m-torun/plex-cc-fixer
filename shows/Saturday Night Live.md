Weekend Update is a mock newscast: the anchors read out a real news item, then joke about it. The viewer hears the news itself from them, so there is no use in telling them beforehand what the anchors are about to say. A card is for what the anchors take for granted: a person, an event or a reference in a story that the viewer might not know and would need in order to get the joke.

The musical guest's songs are just music, with no jokes to get. The viewer likes to know who the guest is; apart from that, the performance needs nothing from the sheet.
